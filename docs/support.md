# Support Fora

Fora is free to use. It is developed and maintained on a voluntary basis, alongside academic work.

If Fora is useful in your research, teaching or projects, you can support its continued development through
GitHub Sponsors:

[:octicons-heart-fill-16:{ .fora-heart } Sponsor on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/otopcu){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

Sponsorship — one-time or monthly — goes toward:

- new IEEE 1516-2025 (HLA 4) capabilities and interoperability verification with independent software;
- keeping the user manuals and programmer guides current;
- the time it takes to answer feedback and fix reported issues.

## Organizations

Organizations can sponsor Fora from their GitHub organization account. For other arrangements, get in touch
through the [Contact page](https://sites.google.com/view/okantopcu/contact).

## Other Ways to Help

- Send feedback, issue reports and ideas through the [Contact page](https://sites.google.com/view/okantopcu/contact).
- Cite Fora in your publications and tell colleagues about it.
- Share your experience of running Fora with other HLA tools.

!!! note "No obligation"

    Sponsorships and donations are voluntary and do not create any warranty, support or service obligation. See the [Disclaimer](disclaimer.md).
